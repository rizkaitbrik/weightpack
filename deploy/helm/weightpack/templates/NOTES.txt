Weightpack is installed: wpkd on the nodes the agent DaemonSet selects, and
wpk-control behind the Service {{ include "weightpack.fullname" . }}.

The OpenAI endpoint, from inside the cluster:

  http://{{ include "weightpack.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.{{ .Values.clusterDomain }}:{{ .Values.control.service.port }}/v1

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "weightpack.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.control.service.port }}
  curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1/models{{ if .Values.auth.apiKey }} -H "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"{{ end }}
